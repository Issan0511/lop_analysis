# leaky_cliff_1111: derivative distribution, functional steps and a finite-budget cliff

Registration 2026-11-11, Codex (stage 4 / leaky a chat). This spec must be committed and pushed before main runs. The user's remembered 0.1 observation may be CondA, not MNIST; this is a NEW experiment, not its claimed replication. No manuscript edits. Historical reanalysis is post hoc.

## Fixed setting
Random-label MNIST, host pmnist_0905 / pmnist_rlmnist_0906: 1200 fixed raw [0,1] training images per seed, 784-100-100-10, batch 16, new iid 10-class labels each task, 80 shuffled epochs = 6000 updates/task, 20 tasks. Native leaky phi_a(z)=where(z>0,z,a*z), same a in both hidden layers. Adam lr=.001, beta=(.9,.999), eps=1e-8, no decay, float32, flush_denormal=True, one CPU thread/process. Same init/subset/label/batch RNG across a. Retain optimizer across natural tasks. Main elapsed resource target: at most 5 CPU workers, reserve 6 GiB RAM; no GPU while it is occupied. No paid worker or chat communication.

Discovery: a={0,.003,.01,.03,.1,.3,1}, seeds 0,1,2. a=0 is the ReLU reference; a=1 is a factorized affine network (not a single linear layer). No LR tuning: inference is conditional on the fixed lr and budget. Save full state after tasks 2,5,10,15,20. Store full per-image/unit z and true/bwd derivative there. Diagnostics at task step 0,1,75,750,1500,...,6000; learning curves each 75 updates (a full epoch).

## Selection and independent confirmation
Order positive nonlinear a values .003,.01,.03,.1,.3. For each adjacent pair compute the absolute difference in seed-mean late (tasks16-20) online accuracy. Choose the largest difference, ties choose smaller lower a. The poorer endpoint is bad, the better good, without assuming monotonicity. Always confirm the two endpoints with NEW seeds 3,4,5,6,7. If discovery difference >=.15, also run the geometric midpoint on those 5 seeds; otherwise omit midpoint and call the absence of a large coarse contrast an outcome. No additional adaptive grid. Confirmatory contrast = paired seed mean late-good minus late-bad; report all seeds and t95% interval (df4), not a binary significance-only conclusion. Discovery not pooled into that interval. Full curves shown separately.

## Metrics (do not conflate)
Online accuracy/CE: pre-update minibatch means over each fixed6000 window. Endpoint: full-bank accuracy/CE at exactly6000 and24000. T90: first evaluated epoch with full-bank accuracy>=.9; right censored, not infinity if unreached. Also T80, continuous CE trajectory and earliest CE<=.5. Natural early/late = tasks1-5/16-20; pair task-end derivative with NEXT-task performance, and also dense within-task trajectory. No average across seeds before temporal event detection.

Each hidden layer separately, inputs first then units: mean signed derivative (nonnegative here, equal to mean absolute), mean square, quantiles, unitwise p+=P_x(z>0), distribution of p+, strictly one-branch units, near-one-branch fraction min(p,1-p)<.01, active width p+>.005 (NOT literal death for a>0), derivative variance within unit, normalized derivative participation (sum_x|g|)^2/(N sum_x g²) with 0 for all-zero; per-image active counts. Report m_i=mean_x z, s_i=sd_x z, r_i=m_i/s_i (undefined s=0 excluded with count), quantiles/means, weights norm. Keep actual forward and surrogate-backward derivatives distinct. No independence claims from exact identities gbar=a+(1-a)p+, g2=a²+(1-a²)p+, var_x g=(1-a)²p(1-p).

At dense sample updates record raw gradient norms, actual floating-point parameter deltas, intended Adam deltas, eps-dominated fraction sqrt(vhat)<eps by layer. On full bank measure actual centered-logit delta, analytic true-forward J delta (all parameters and hidden-only), error alignment -<p-y,J delta>/N, normalized alignment, actual full-bank CE decrease and linear-predicted decrease. At the initial step also show shadow uniform gradient scaling c=.1,.01 with (i) stale moments, (ii) consistently scaled whole history m->c*m,v->c²*v, (iii) fresh moments; do not conflate that controlled algebra with a change that alters gradient directions. Gradients=0 need not mean zero Adam movement if moments remain. Learning-rate ratio is not inferred from mean phi'.

## Hypotheses and falsifiers
Hmean: a common mean-derivative cutoff explains failures. Counterexample if overlapping mean derivative (within .02 per layer) accompanies substantial differences (> .20 endpoint accuracy) at matched task/budget, or B/X effects cannot be ordered by mean. These are descriptive disconfirmations, not a fully identified replacement theory. Distinguish continuous distribution drift from collective loss: per-seed steepest natural online drop and aligned dense p+/derivative distribution/m,s,r/functional updates; no pooled-seed cliff.
Hbudget: at6000 there is a gap which contracts by>=50% at24000, or N reaches.9 after6000; this supports budget-sensitive slow adaptation for those branches, not global attainability. A persistent gap at24000 is unresolved, not proof of insufficient capacity.
Hmoment: O over N improves6000 endpoint by>=.15 (paired mean) with corresponding functional-step recovery, supports optimizer-history cost. H over O diagnoses additional head-reset effect; does not isolate forward geometry alone.
Hsignal: B improves endpoint by>=.15 with initial forward preserved, and reverse B lowers it by>=.15, supports a role for backward signal in this box. BO vs O and B vs N reveal history interaction. If X helps but B does not, forward response matters; if A only helps its initial function shift is a confound. B can damage alignment; report rather than assume benefit.
Hnonlinear: high a does not imply high adaptation; a1 and one-sided fractions are controls. A loss of folding/usable directions is a candidate only, not proved by rank/one-sidedness or positive gradient alone. Random labels require useful task directions, not weight motion.
Phase transition: no thermodynamic transition, absorption, bifurcation or hysteresis will be claimed from these finite trajectories. a>0 does not have ReLU's literal zero derivative absorption. A sharp finite-width crossover can be reported; finite-size or hysteresis follow-up only if observed sharpness/same-state dependence motivates it.
