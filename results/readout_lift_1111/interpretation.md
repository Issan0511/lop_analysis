# Interpretation of readout_lift_1111

The requested causal question was why a smaller readout can induce larger unit outputs although sinking persists. The strongest supported answer in this one-hidden-layer ELU setting is selective fitting to persistent new labels under a changed balance of output demands. Readout regrowth and a shallower negative-body trajectory are not required for upper-tail growth in the tested controls. This does not establish a universal plasticity benefit or an additive mediation percentage.

The exact activity gradient is D_i=sum_c p_c V_ci-V_yi. A unit whose column most strongly supports the correct class always receives a nonpositive D on that example. Different examples of the same unit can have opposing demands. Splitting D into V^T(p-u) and V^T(u-y) distinguishes a uniform-target term from the persistent-label term. Reducing V changes probabilities as well as the backward multiplier. Pure backward scaling at fixed residual preserves signs; changing probabilities can reverse them.

At V01's task10 endpoint, the mean D over its current top5% inputs is -.010214. Keeping these features fixed and using the probabilities from a mean-compensated10x readout changes it to+.001713 with the original backward map. Changing only the backward map instead gives-.102138. Changing both gives+.017135. Both tasks and every seed show the same sign pattern. This is a local matched-state diagnostic, not a claim that every input changes sign or that all upward magnitudes increase.

With a frozen0.1 readout and ordinary updates, the median unit maximum increases from8.380 to14.277 at task10 and9.508 to17.693 at task25. Uniform targets or labels resampled per occurrence remove this expansion. In exact-gradient body-constrained controls, all arms share the original-large-readout body's full measured trajectory; body changes at4000 are-1.253 and-.887. Yet small fixed readouts with persistent labels reach maxima18.257 and22.149, compared with7.857/9.247 for full readouts, -1.165/-.964 for uniform targets, and3.164/3.391 for fresh labels. Registered contrasts hold in all3seeds at both tasks. The coordinates' W Adam state is cleared in all four follow-up arms; this is not the natural Q trajectory.

The first matched-body control failed an important criterion. Correcting only hidden bias after an unconstrained Adam step made the uniform-target arm develop very large activity and poor loss. That arm and the failed P4 remain in the report. A follow-up was preregistered AFTER observing this failure and BEFORE its own outcomes. It differentiates the actual constrained forward, z=W(x-xbar_body)+m_ref(t). Its uniform-target loss is stable and its hard-label versus uniform/fresh-label contrasts pass. External forcing is still present, so neither constraint intervention provides a clean percentage of natural Q's total effect.

Actual observed-denominator Adam source accounting supports the label-driven direction. In the original fixed upper5% group, task10's late probes give a label contribution+.018730 and uniform contribution-.019622 with full fixed V; small fixed V gives+.004124 and-.003891. Thus the upward component is smaller, while the balance becomes positive. The source terms are not causal deletion effects.

Input identity also changes. With frozen V, the label term for any fixed input/unit is time-invariant, but its mean among CURRENT top5% inputs shifts from+.000427 to-.026130 in task10 V01. Persistent-label learning selects new upper-tail inputs. Post-hoc normalized class-support and overlap audits corroborate this; label-free controls do not show the same correct-class enrichment. This distinguishes selective response growth from a uniform increase in every input's output.

Larger activity is not itself improved learning: task10's fixed small readout can have larger maxima but lower accuracy than the large one. Q with both layers learning is a separate condition. Old September task151-200 summaries also show within-task top recovery while net per-task sinking remains; a monotonically rising upper tail over long training is not established.
